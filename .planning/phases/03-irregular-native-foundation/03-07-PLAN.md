<objective>
Build the one flow in this phase that exists because of a clinical risk rather than a product goal:
the 90-day sentinel. A woman whose endometrium has not shed in three months has a real and
under-communicated risk, and almost nothing in her life is going to tell her that.

Purpose: this is the flow where the product's two commitments pull against each other and have to
be reconciled deliberately. CYCL-02 forbids pregnancy-scare language anywhere in the daily
experience. CYCL-06 requires asking whether pregnancy is possible before saying anything else. Both
are correct: the daily cycle view never raises pregnancy, and the escalation asks it once, as
triage, because the answer changes what the rest of the flow should say. That reconciliation is a
design decision, and it belongs in the copy Satheesh reviews.

Output: the sentinel, the triage questions, the education, the export, and the snoozed reminder.

Owners: Dhodraj Sundaram on the sentinel and the export payload; Belson Johnson on the screens;
Satheeshkumar reviews the copy, which his role scope names explicitly.
</objective>

  <files>src/core/cycle/sentinel.ts, tests/cycle/sentinel.test.ts</files>
  <behavior>
    - A gap of 91 days since the last period or withdrawal bleed raises the sentinel.
    - A gap of 89 days does not.
    - Three bleeds in the trailing year raises the sentinel even when the current gap is short,
      because the fewer-than-four-a-year condition is independent of the gap condition.
    - Four bleeds in the trailing year with a short gap does not raise it.
    - Answering the hormonal-medication question writes `USER.onHormonalMedication`, and the
      classifier's state changes accordingly on the next read.
    - Once raised and acknowledged, the sentinel schedules a follow-up between 14 and 28 days out.
    - If a period is logged before the follow-up fires, the follow-up is cancelled.
    - Snoozing pushes the follow-up out again rather than cancelling it, and a snooze is always
      available.
    - The follow-up payload is constructed through the notification gate as a due action.
  </behavior>
  <action>
    Two independent trigger conditions, both from CYCL-06: a gap over 90 days, and fewer than four
    bleeds in a year. Implement them as two conditions, not one. A woman bleeding three times a year
    at roughly 100-day intervals trips the gap check; a woman bleeding three times a year in an
    irregular clump may not, and she is the person the second condition exists for.

    Use the same 90-day boundary the classifier uses for amenorrhea. Two thresholds that are meant
    to be the same number and are written in two places will drift; import one.

    Model the flow as a small explicit state machine — not raised, raised, triage answered,
    acknowledged, snoozed, resolved — using a discriminated union. Research assessed a full state
    machine library as overkill at this scale and recommended a reducer or union, which is the right
    call here.

    Persist the triage answers. The hormonal-medication answer writes `USER.onHormonalMedication`,
    which is the field the classifier needs to reach two of its six states and which research
    identified as impossible to derive from interval data. The escalation is the most reliable place
    that answer gets collected, so wire it through properly rather than treating it as flow-local.

    The pregnancy answer is triage: it changes what the flow shows next and it is not stored as a
    tracked variable, is not used in any statistic, and is not surfaced anywhere else in the product.
    Recording it as a persistent user attribute would turn a one-time clinical question into exactly
    the pregnancy-tracking the product does not do.

    Schedule the follow-up between 14 and 28 days out through plan 03-06's scheduler, constructing
    the payload through the gate as a due action. Do not call the native notification bridge
    directly — routing around the gate is how the daily cap silently stops applying. Cancel on a
    logged period. Snooze pushes it out; snooze is always offered.

    Write `tests/cycle/sentinel.test.ts` first, with boundary fixtures at 89, 90 and 91 days and at
    three and four bleeds per year, and the notification bridge mocked.
  </action>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| sentinel state -> notification | A clinical escalation becomes text that may render on a lock screen |
| health records -> exported document | Her full cycle history leaves the app in a shareable file |
| triage answer -> persistent user attribute | A one-time clinical question could become tracked data if stored carelessly |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-28 | Information Disclosure | escalation notification text | high | mitigate | The follow-up payload is a due action that references the reminder without naming the cycle gap in the body, so a lock-screen preview does not disclose her cycle state to someone else holding the phone. |
| T-03-29 | Information Disclosure | triage pregnancy answer | high | mitigate | The pregnancy answer is flow-local triage, never persisted as a user attribute and never used in any statistic — storing it would create pregnancy tracking the product does not do. |
| T-03-30 | Information Disclosure | generated export file | medium | mitigate | Generated client-side and handed directly to the platform share sheet; no upload path and no temporary copy left outside the app sandbox after sharing. |
| T-03-31 | Tampering | escalation copy | critical | mitigate | Escalation strings are literal frozen constants asserted by test; no runtime composition and no generated text on a path carrying clinical weight. |
| T-03-32 | Tampering | notification cap bypass | medium | mitigate | The follow-up schedules through plan 03-06's gate and scheduler rather than calling the native bridge directly, so it inherits the daily cap and quiet hours. |
</threat_model>
