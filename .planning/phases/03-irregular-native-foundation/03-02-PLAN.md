<objective>
Expand the tracer's three-table schema into the full Phase 3 entity set, and build the rule-based
cycle intelligence that makes an irregular cycle readable as signal instead of error: interval
statistics, the six-state classifier, and probabilistic phase estimation.

Purpose: this is the layer that turns "irregular-native" from a copy promise into a computation.
It is also the layer most at risk of scope creep — the statistical n-of-1 engine is Phase 4 and
must not be pulled forward into it.

Output: Dexie schema version 2 with every Phase 3 entity, zod validators at the write boundary, and
three pure, unit-tested modules over her own history.

Owner: Dhodraj Sundaram. Satheeshkumar pairs on Task 3, where schema and product logic intersect —
specifically on whether each of the six states means to a user what the classifier says it means.
</objective>

      is statistically indistinguishable from a natural one on interval data alone.
    - A user with a hormonal-discontinuation date inside the recovery window classifies as post-pill

      value rather than guessing.

    since her last bleed, and the medication context from the USER row. Returns one of the six named
