<objective>
Freeze the three interface contracts. This is the single decision the weekend rests on: build-plan.md
section 5 says so plainly, and section 12 records that if Belson is ever blocked waiting on the
engine, the types were not frozen properly.

Plan 01-01 put a provisional two-variant `Finding` in place to prove the toolchain reaches a phone.
Plan 01-04 produced a field-level list of what five real screens actually need. This plan replaces the
provisional shape with the real one, adds `LogEntry` and `ExportPayload`, and locks the barrel.

The honesty of the whole product lives in one property of one type: a finding must be able to say
*not enough data* as a value, not as an `if` statement written at three in the morning. The existing
`INSIGHT` entity in `product/04-data-model.md` gets this wrong with a boolean `surfaced` flag bolted
onto an always-present shape. That precedent is rejected here. The `VERDICT.result` entity in the same
file, which already treats no-detectable-change as a first-class value, is the precedent extended.

Owner: **Dhodraj Sundaram** writes the types — build-plan.md section 5 assigns all three to him by
name. **Satheeshkumar** writes the test harness that proves the exhaustiveness guarantee is real, which
is his pairing role. Neither Belson nor Rahul touches `src/types/` in this plan.

Purpose: give Belson a surface he can build six screens against without one conversation, and give the
engine a shape to emit into. Output: a frozen, typechecking, exhaustiveness-guarded `src/types/`.
</objective>

   threshold and display "still learning", never surface a pattern from insufficient observations —
