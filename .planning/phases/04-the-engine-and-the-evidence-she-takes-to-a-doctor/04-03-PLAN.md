<objective>
The second vertical slice of this phase: she taps once and holds a document a clinician can read in
seven minutes. Eighteen months of cycle history, the interval statistics, the bleeding log, a
symptom timeline, her medication log, and the five tests she can name when she asks for them — as a
PDF composed and rendered entirely on her phone, in under ten seconds, with every value the app
derived labelled a wellness trend rather than a clinical measurement.

Purpose: this is the artifact that changes what happens in the consultation. It is a sibling slice
to the insight reveal, not a layer on top of it — it shares only the Dexie read, and it carries its
own end-to-end verify from tap to share sheet.

A note on where this runs, because "Worker" is overloaded in this project: DOC-01 requires the PDF
to be generated client-side, so it is composed and rendered on the device, inside a **Web Worker**
off the UI thread. Nothing about the document's contents crosses to the Cloudflare Worker. Rahul's
infrastructure work here is the on-device side of that — the Vite worker bundling and code-split
that keeps jsPDF out of the cold-start path, the Capacitor plugin sync, and the private-directory
placement — plus the Cloudflare Worker deploy path in Plan 04-04.

Output: the export compiler, the named-test constants, the labelling path, the renderer, its
performance guard, the native save-and-share bridge, the one-tap trigger and the result screen.
</objective>

interface ExportPayload {

The `disclaimer` literal is a required field precisely so FR-504 cannot be typed around. If the
frozen file on disk differs, the frozen file wins — extend it additively, never narrow it.

From `src/db/schema.ts` (Phase 3): Dexie tables for cycles, bleed events, check-ins with symptom
entries, medications and consent. CYCL-04 distinguishes period, spotting, withdrawal bleed and
breakthrough bleeding; CYCL-05 maintains the rolling interval distribution and bleeds-per-year
counter — read those rather than recomputing a second definition here.

Installed by Plan 04-01: `jspdf`, `jspdf-autotable`, `@capacitor/filesystem@6`, `@capacitor/share@6`.

Budget: PDF within 10 seconds (NFR-105), bundle under 60 MB, cold start under 2.5 s on a
₹10,000-class Android with 2 GB RAM.
