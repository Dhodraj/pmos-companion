<objective>
Deliver the screening instrument that runs on cadence and the one response that must route her to
help immediately.

Purpose: SAFE-01 names PHQ-4 and GAD-2. SAFE-05 requires immediate escalation on any suicidality
response. As specified those two cannot both be satisfied — PHQ-4 (PHQ-2 + GAD-2) excludes the
suicidal-ideation item by construction, so there is no field in the named instrument that a
suicidality response could ever populate. This plan closes that gap with a supplementary safety-net
item, and routes the instrument choice through a real clinical decision rather than making it in
code.

Output: a screening flow that scores on device, stores on device, fires on cadence, and hands a
single affirmative safety-net response straight to Plan 01's crisis screen.

Dhodraj Sundaram (dhodraj) — on-device store, scoring, escalation wiring.
Satheeshkumar (SatheeshKumarSSK) — coordinates the instrument decision and QA of the item wording.
</objective>

## Decisions this plan locks

**D5-F — PHQ-4 alone cannot satisfy SAFE-05, and this is named rather than worked around.** PHQ-4 is
the ultra-brief caseness screener; item 9 of the full PHQ-9 — the suicidal-ideation item — is
excluded from it by design. A supplementary safety-net item is added specifically so SAFE-05's
escalation trigger has a source. This is new clinical-instrument scope beyond the "PHQ-4 and GAD-2,
monthly or user-initiated" wording the reviewing clinician approved (CON-CLIN-05), so it is not
treated as pre-approved: Task 1 gates it on a real clinical decision, and Plan 06 carries it into
the REG-03 sign-off packet.

**D5-G — The escalation trigger is a single affirmative response, not persistence, and not the
caseness cutoff.** CON-CLIN-05 is explicit: immediate escalation on any suicidality or self-harm
response. Reusing the PHQ-2/GAD-2 cutoff of 3 as the escalation trigger is forbidden — that cutoff
answers "is she likely depressed or anxious", a different question, and conflating them either
alarms everyone who screens positive for depression or never fires on real risk.

**D5-H — Responses and scores stay on device.** Item responses and computed scores are written to
IndexedDB via Dexie only, in the same tier as the existing check-in entities. The only thing that
crosses the network is Plan 01's count-only event (ADR-004, NFR-901, NFR-903).

## Candidate instrument wording (for Task 1's decision)

**Option A — PHQ-9 item 9, verbatim.** Free of copyright restriction including commercial use.
Stem: `Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by any of the following problems?`
Item: `Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself in some way`
Responses: `Not at all` (0) / `Several days` (1) / `More than half the days` (2) / `Nearly every day` (3).
Escalation fires on any response scoring 1 or above.

**Option B — NIMH Ask Suicide-Screening Questions (ASQ), 4 items.** Free, independently validated,
but a second instrument family alongside the PHQ family already in the product.

## Frozen instrument item text (SAFE-01)

Stem for all four caseness items: `Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the
following problems?` Responses: `Not at all` (0) / `Several days` (1) / `More than half the days`
(2) / `Nearly every day` (3).

| Key | Item text | Subscale |
|---|---|---|
| `phq2_1` | `Little interest or pleasure in doing things` | depression |
| `phq2_2` | `Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless` | depression |
| `gad2_1` | `Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge` | anxiety |
| `gad2_2` | `Not being able to stop or control worrying` | anxiety |

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| screening form → on-device store | Her item responses cross into persistence; this boundary must not extend to the network |
| screening submit → crisis escalation | The safety-critical decision boundary — a wrong predicate here either over-alarms or misses real risk |
| on-device store → Worker | Only the count-only event may cross; responses and scores may not |
