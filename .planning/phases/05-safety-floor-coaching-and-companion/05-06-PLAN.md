<objective>
Close the two release gates this phase cannot ship without: proof that nothing in the deployment can
stand between her and the phone number, and a real human sign-off on everything the phase says.

Purpose: SAFE-03's guarantee is only half-owned by application code. Plan 01 made the crisis screen
incapable of being rate-limited by removing its network dependency; this plan makes the deployment
incapable of gating the path that serves it, and wires that into CI so a future deploy cannot quietly
reintroduce a gate. REG-03 is the other gate: a qualified clinician must sign off the health content
and a mental-health professional must sign off the crisis content before any of it reaches a user.
That is a hard gate on this phase, not a formality — the phase does not complete without a signed,
dated record in the repository.

Output: the deploy-config audit and its CI job, the assembled sign-off packet, and the signed
`SIGNOFF.md`.

crisis path, the CI gate.
Satheeshkumar (SatheeshKumarSSK) — assembles the packet, runs it past the reviewers, records the
sign-off, and QAs the crisis copy against `hackathon/clinician-review-brief.md`.

## Decisions this plan locks

**D5-Y — The crisis path is served statically and excluded from Worker invocation.** Cloudflare Pages
decides per route whether the Worker runs. Excluding the crisis route means the crisis screen is
served from the static bundle by the CDN, so it survives a Worker outage, a failed Worker deploy and
a Worker quota exhaustion. Combined with Plan 01's zero-network render path, there is no component
whose failure removes the phone number.

**D5-Z — The crisis path is the one documented exception to access control.** Every other Worker
endpoint this phase adds sits behind whatever access control Phases 3–4 established. The crisis
screen and the path serving it deliberately do not, and the exception is recorded in the
configuration itself so a later security review reads it as intentional rather than as an oversight
to be corrected.

**D5-AA — REG-03 is a blocking gate with a repository artifact, not an approval in a conversation.**
The sign-off is a checked-in `SIGNOFF.md` naming each reviewer, their credential, what they reviewed
and the date. A release-checklist step verifies its presence. An unsigned phase does not ship.

**D5-AB — Four items go to the reviewers as explicit open questions, not as approved content.** These
are the places where this phase added scope beyond what was previously reviewed, and they are
surfaced rather than buried:
1. The safety-net suicidality item added to PHQ-4 and GAD-2 (Plan 02, D5-F) — new clinical-instrument
   scope beyond the "PHQ-4 and GAD-2" wording of CON-CLIN-05.
2. The eating-disorder screen instrument and its licensing for commercial use (Plan 04, D5-N), plus
   the tension between its weight-change item and the opt-in weight policy (D5-O).
3. The three-tier evidence-grade labels and their mapping back to GRADE and USPSTF (Plan 03, D5-I).
4. The deliberate omission of an emergency-services number from the crisis screen (Plan 01, D5-D) —
   included or omitted is the reviewer's call, not the team's.
