<objective>
Make the surfaces most likely to hurt her structurally incapable of doing so.

Purpose: body-image distress is worse in this cohort than in controls on every MBSRQ-AS subscale
after BMI adjustment, and food-tracking depth is the highest-risk surface in the product. These five
requirements are all absence requirements — the app must not count energy intake, must not show
energy expenditure, must not ask her weight unprompted, must not rate her appearance, and must not
open food depth to someone who screens positive for an eating disorder. Absence enforced by policy
regresses; absence enforced by the data model and by a CI audit does not.

Output: a default-closed food-depth gate behind an eating-disorder screen, food and movement models
with no energy-arithmetic field at all, opt-in-gated weight entry, and an audit suite that fails CI
if any appearance-rating surface ever appears.

Dhodraj Sundaram (dhodraj) — the data models and the absence audits.
Satheeshkumar (SatheeshKumarSSK) — carries the screen's instrument and wording into Plan 06's
sign-off packet.

## Decisions this plan locks

**D5-M — The food-depth gate is default-closed and has three states, not two.** `not-screened`,
`screened-negative` and `screened-positive`. Depth opens only on `screened-negative`. A user who has
never taken the screen sits in `not-screened` and sees the same additive, cuisine-native surface a
positive screen would leave her with. SAFE-04's gating is then correct from first launch and does
not depend on the screen having been prompted.

**D5-N — Proposed eating-disorder screen: SCOFF, five items, positive at two or more.** No new
instrument is invented — inventing screening logic has no validation behind it and raises rather
than lowers CDSCO classification risk (CON-CLIN-08). The five items, as they will be rendered:

| Key | Item text |
|---|---|
| `scoff_1` | `Do you make yourself sick because you feel uncomfortably full?` |
| `scoff_2` | `Do you worry you have lost control over how much you eat?` |
| `scoff_3` | `Have you recently lost more than 6.35 kg (one stone) in a three-month period?` |
| `scoff_4` | `Do you believe yourself to be fat when others say you are too thin?` |
| `scoff_5` | `Would you say that food dominates your life?` |

Each is yes/no; two or more yes responses is a positive screen. Two things about this instrument go
into Plan 06's REG-03 sign-off packet as explicit questions rather than being assumed: whether SCOFF
is the instrument the reviewing clinician wants, and whether its licensing permits commercial use in
this product. Until that returns, the gate ships default-closed, which is the safe direction.

**D5-O — SCOFF item 3 asks about weight change; this is not a weight-tracking feature.** Answering a
one-off screening question is not the same as entering weight into a log. The response is scored and
discarded with the rest of the screen's responses; no weight value is derived, stored or displayed
from it, and it does not open the COACH-05 weight surface. This tension is flagged for the sign-off
clinician.

**D5-P — The food and movement models have no energy-arithmetic field at all.** Not a hidden field,
not a computed-but-unrendered value, not a field behind a feature flag. COACH-03 and COACH-04 require
the system never to count, not merely never to show. A value that exists is one refactor away from
being rendered. For the same reason no nutrition database or nutrition API is integrated — those are
energy-shaped at the data-model level.

**D5-Q — On a positive screen, support is surfaced, not a crisis escalation.** A positive
eating-disorder screen is not one of SAFE-05's five red flags and must not route to the crisis
screen. It suppresses depth and surfaces a supportive, non-alarming pointer to talk to a clinician.

**D5-R — The eating-disorder screen result is never sent to D1.** Unlike the SAFE-05 crisis path,
NFR-903 does not ask for eating-disorder screen counts, and a server-side record that an account
screened positive for an eating disorder is the kind of data ADR-004 exists to prevent. The result
is an on-device gating flag and nothing else.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| eating-disorder screen result → food surfaces | A clinical screening outcome crosses into feature gating; it must gate correctly and must not travel further |
| on-device gate state → network | The boundary this plan forbids entirely for eating-disorder screen results (D5-R) |
| per-class consent row → weight surfaces | Weight rendering must depend on the consent row and on nothing else |
