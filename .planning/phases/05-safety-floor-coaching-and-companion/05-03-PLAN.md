<objective>
Make it impossible for the app to tell her to do something without also telling her how well that
advice is supported and when it was last checked.

Purpose: COACH-02 requires a visible evidence grade and review date on every health claim. Enforcing
that by review discipline fails the first time someone adds a card in a hurry. This plan enforces it
by type: the content record cannot be constructed without both fields, and a content-lint test fails
CI if any user-facing claim in the repo sits outside that type. COACH-01's daily delivery then rides
on top of a deck that is graded by construction.

Output: the graded-claim type and three-tier grade vocabulary the whole app uses, the curated
micro-action deck, deterministic daily selection, and the card that renders the grade and the date.

**Owners:** Satheeshkumar (SatheeshKumarSSK) — the evidence-grade vocabulary, the curated deck's
content and its mapping back to GRADE/USPSTF, and QA that no card overstates its evidence.
Belson Johnson (Belsonselva) — the card and the daily-actions surface.
</objective>

## Decisions this plan locks

**D5-I — Three consumer tiers, each mapped back to an established clinical framework.** An invented
scale gives the REG-03 sign-off clinician nothing standard to check against. The tiers, their
on-screen labels and their source mapping:

| Tier | On-screen label | Maps back to |
|---|---|---|
| `strong` | `Strong evidence` | GRADE high certainty; USPSTF A or B |
| `moderate` | `Moderate evidence` | GRADE moderate certainty; USPSTF C |
| `limited` | `Limited evidence` | GRADE low or very low certainty; USPSTF I (insufficient) |

The exact label wording is proposed here and goes to the REG-03 clinician in Plan 06's sign-off
packet. The mapping column ships in code as data so the reviewer can audit any card back to its
framework tier.

**D5-J — Forbidden claim wording.** No badge may use a star rating, a percentage of effectiveness, a
numeric score, the phrase "clinically proven", the phrase "doctor recommended", or any cure, reverse,
regulate or restore-fertility language. These are e-commerce trust-badge conventions with no
relationship to strength of evidence, and the last group is prohibited outright by DMR Act 1954,
FSSAI and ASCI (CON-CLIN-07, NFR-703). Task 3 enforces this list as a lint over the content set.

**D5-K — Phase awareness never asserts a phase.** CYCL-07 estimates cycle phase probabilistically
with visible uncertainty and forbids hard prediction. Phase-aware selection therefore filters the
deck by estimated phase only when the phase estimate carries enough confidence; below that it falls
back to phase-neutral actions. The card never prints the phase as a fact, and no action's text
depends on the phase being right.

**D5-L — The deck degrades to zero labs and zero sensor data.** No micro-action may require a
laboratory value, a camera reading or a weight entry to be deliverable (ADR-003, CON-FEAS-06,
COACH-05).

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| curated content → user | Authored health content crosses to a user who will act on it; the only control is the grade, the date and the wording lint |
| cycle-phase estimate → action selection | An uncertain probabilistic estimate crosses into content selection and must not be laundered into apparent certainty |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-13 | Tampering | A health claim shipping without a grade or review date | high | mitigate | `HealthClaim` makes both fields non-optional at the type level; `tests/content/claim-wording-lint.test.ts` pass one audits every claim in the repo on every CI run |
| T-05-14 | Spoofing | A badge implying stronger evidence than the underlying framework tier | high | mitigate | `GRADE_SOURCE_MAPPING` ships as auditable data mapping each tier back to GRADE and USPSTF; pass two lints the forbidden marketing and outcome-claim wording from D5-J |
| T-05-15 | Tampering | Regulated outcome claims (cure, reverse, regulate, restore fertility) entering content | high | mitigate | Pass two of the wording lint blocks those verbs in CI; NFR-703 and DMR Act 1954 reach the whole content chain |
| T-05-16 | Information Disclosure | Phase-aware selection revealing an inferred cycle phase as fact | medium | mitigate | D5-K gates phase filtering on the CYCL-07 confidence threshold and falls back to phase-neutral entries; the card prints no phase |
| T-05-17 | Repudiation | Stale review dates going unnoticed as guidelines update | medium | accept | Review dates are visible to the user and auditable in the deck, but automated staleness alerting is NFR-705 re-review work scheduled against the NICE December 2026 publication, not a Phase 5 deliverable |
</threat_model>
