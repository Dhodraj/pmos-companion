<objective>
Turn the tracer's two-control check-in into the real thirty-second check-in, and the tracer's
single-variant insight card into one that renders every Finding variant honestly — both built
entirely against frozen types and hand-written fixtures.

Purpose: build-plan.md §5 calls this "the single most valuable thing the pre-week produces" — once
the types are frozen, Belson builds every screen against fixtures and never waits for the engine.
This plan is where that pays off: it runs in the same wave as the engine (plan 02-04) and shares no
file with it. If the H10 blind test goes red and rings 2 to 4 are abandoned, the check-in and the
card are what survives, because build-plan.md §10 says the spine is never cut.

Output: a check-in a judge can complete in thirty seconds, and a card that says "still learning" as
readily as it states a pattern.

Owner: **Belson Johnson**, all three tasks. Frontend only — he touches nothing under `src/core/`,
`worker/` or `ops/` (build-plan.md §2). Dhodraj writes none of this, not even one card
(build-plan.md §3, rule two).
</objective>
